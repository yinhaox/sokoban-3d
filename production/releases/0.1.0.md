## Highlights

- **3D room and isometric camera** — a rotating, zooming view with walls that fade only when they stand between the camera and the player (`#8`, `#10`).
- **Playable loop** — move, push, undo (100 steps), restart and a 1–3 star result, with animated transitions (`#12`).
- **Menu, HUD, pause and victory shell** — a screen router, keyboard-navigable menus, confirmations for destructive actions (`#14`).
- **Twenty-level campaign** — teaching levels, difficulty curve and solver-verified levels (`#19`).
- **Save, progression, settings and EN/ZH** — atomic save with migration and corruption recovery, unlocked levels, volume, language, camera speed, colour-blind palette, reduced motion, fullscreen (`#18`).
- **Runtime-synthesized audio** — seven procedural cues wired to gameplay and buttons, with settings-driven buses and no binary audio assets (`#23`).
- **Visual polish** — faint floor grid, soft contact shadows, a dark base under the room, pale translucent faded walls with merged contours instead of black holes (`#29`, `#31`).
- **Headless rule layer** — every rule and level parses and plays without a window (`#4`).
- **Boot-only debug arguments** for state-specific screenshots and timed runs (`#22`).
- **Design set and corrections** — twelve pages, reconciled with implementation (`#2`, `#7`, `#28`).
- **Packaging and CI** — Windows/Linux/Web export presets, README, licenses, and a Godot CI job that runs the headless suite (`2d70c1b`, `01b22b5`).

## Fixes

- **Room framing and near-wall fade** (`#10`): the room is centred with equal opposite margins, faded walls keep a visible outline, and the goal plate no longer looks like it sits outside the wall ring.
- **Faded walls read as black holes with noisy seams** (`#31`): complete pale top faces at 25% alpha, one merged contour per wall run instead of eight per cell, and floor under wall cells so the ghost shows floor tone rather than the dark base.

## Known issues

- **Audio is placeholder quality**: seven runtime-synthesized cues, not a designed sound set. Replacing them means editing the cue table or the director, not gameplay code.
- **`--screen=play` without `--level` opens level 1**, not the last played level; pass `--level=<1..20|level-010>`.
- **No store assets and no code signing**: no capsule art, store copy or marketing images; the Windows build is unsigned, so SmartScreen warns on first run.
- **Crates and goals differ by colour and shape**, but the colour-blind palette is deuteranopia-oriented only; other deficiencies are not tuned.
- **One deployment, one repository**: the studio that built this ran against this repository only, on Windows, with a desktop session for rendering.
