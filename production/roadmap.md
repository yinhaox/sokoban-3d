# Roadmap

A plan, not a promise. The producer updates this file; everyone else reads it.

| Milestone | Target date | Status | What ships | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| _M1 Playable core_ | 2026-03-06 | planned | _One line: the slice a player can try._ | producer |
| _M2 First vertical slice_ | 2026-04-17 | planned | _One line: what becomes real._ | producer |

Status values:

- planned: not started.
- in progress: at least one story is in-progress or done.
- done: every story is done and its evidence index reports Run result: OBSERVED.
- cut: dropped or moved out of the current plan.

Rules:

- Target dates are ISO 8601 dates written as YYYY-MM-DD.
- A milestone moves to done only when its stories are done and their evidence indexes exist.
- Changing a date is a decision for the producer, not for a worker ticket.
- Release notes live in production/releases/, not here.
